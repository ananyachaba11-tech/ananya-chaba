About Me Data Stories

I’m Ananya, a data analyst transitioning into data journalism, with a focus on reporting, coding and visual storytelling.

I grew up in Faridabad, a city in the National Capital Region that has always felt much smaller than neighbouring Delhi. I studied economics at Ashoka University.

Economics gave me a way to make sense of questions I had carried as a young adult—through incentives, constraints and trade-offs—and taught me to look for the systems underneath the things we see around us. At Ashoka, I also worked on behavioural research and information design as a thesis student.

I then spent several years in India’s development sector, first working on gender, primary data collection and analysis at the Udaiti Foundation, and later on public health, data modelling and analysis at the Clinton Health Access Initiative (CHAI).

All my work has led me to where I am today: wanting to bring together my knack for writing and storytelling, my skills in data wrangling and analysis, and my understanding of public systems to make data more accessible and use it to deepen how we understand India.

I like to find data where little or none exists, figure out how to structure and analyse it, ask the right questions, and communicate the insights through clear and simple visualisations.

While I’ve largely taught myself to code and work with data, my experiences at Ashoka, Udaiti, CHAI and Columbia Journalism School have continually sharpened those skills.

In all my work, I try to hold myself to high standards of methodological rigour, integrity and transparency.

I am currently based in Delhi. You can contact me at ananyachaba11@gmail.com.
